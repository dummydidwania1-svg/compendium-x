# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Aspiring management consultants (primarily Indian college students and early-career professionals) preparing for case interviews. They practice solo or in pairs, typically on a laptop at a desk. Both candidates and interviewers use the platform; each person opens a separate browser tab or window during a live session. Users range from first-timers exploring case frameworks to experienced practitioners refining their technique.

## Product Purpose

Compendium X is a structured case interview practice platform. It gives aspiring consultants access to a curated library of cases, a live two-person session environment with audio recording and AI transcription, and a personal performance dashboard with scoring, trends, and feedback analytics. Success looks like a user improving their case performance measurably over repeated sessions — visible through score trends on the dashboard.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, warm, editorial. The platform takes consulting seriously without being cold or corporate — it feels like a well-curated study guide, not a sterile SaaS tool.

## Anti-references

- Brash SaaS dashboards (Mixpanel, Amplitude) — too data-heavy and corporate
- Generic mock-interview apps that feel like quiz tools
- Overly gamified platforms with streaks, confetti, and achievement badges
- Minimalist-to-the-point-of-sterile design (no personality)

## Design Principles

1. **The practice room, not the software** — UI should disappear into the task. A candidate mid-case should be thinking about frameworks, not fighting the interface.
2. **Earned warmth** — The editorial warmth (Newsreader serif, cream palette, forest green) signals credibility, not coziness. Every surface reinforces that this is a serious preparation tool.
3. **Clarity at a glance** — Dashboard and feedback surfaces must communicate performance status in seconds. Dense information is fine; ambiguous information is not.
4. **Consistent vocabulary** — Same component shapes, same interaction patterns, same color roles across every screen. The interviewer view and the candidate view should feel like the same product.
5. **Graceful at every size** — The 13-inch MacBook is the design origin; every other screen size should receive the same intent, never a broken or cramped subset of it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. Reduced-motion alternatives for all animations. Touch targets ≥44px for tablet and mobile users. Color is never the sole carrier of meaning (score states use both color and label/icon).
